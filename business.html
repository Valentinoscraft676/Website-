FAQ.

Check out frequently asked questions

  • 1. What kind of projects can you estimate?

    I provide estimates for a wide range of home projects, including flooring, painting, fencing, storage units, driveways, and general renovations. If it’s around the house, I can usually give guidance.

  • 2. How accurate are your estimates?

    All estimates are calculated using real measurements, material specifications, and a small allowance for waste or adjustments. They are designed to give a clear picture of costs and quantities before you start.

  • 3. Do you provide advice on materials and layouts?

    Yes! Part of my service is giving practical advice on materials, spacing, and layout to make your project more efficient and cost-effective.

  • 4. Can you help with planning multiple projects at once?

    Absolutely. I can look at your whole property and suggest how to sequence projects, make the most of your space, and avoid unnecessary costs.

  • 5. How do I provide my measurements?

    You can send measurements via email, photos, or notes. I can also provide guidance on how to measure accurately if you're unsure.
